WiFi link Off

Sends up to 10 tiny requests a second to your router and times each round trip. A body moving near the path between this device and the router changes the signal, and with it the timing. Chrome or Edge asks once to allow local network access.

Any device on your network with a web page works, such as a printer or NAS. Most routers answer at their admin address.

Bluetooth Off

Optional. Listens to the signal strength of phones, watches, speakers and other devices advertising nearby; a body in the way weakens them. Bluetooth mode needs Chrome's experimental flag: open chrome://flags/#enable-experimental-web-platform-features, set it to Enabled and relaunch Chrome.
