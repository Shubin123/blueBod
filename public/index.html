WiFi sensing boards ＋

Flash an ESP32 or ESP32-S3 with Espressif's esp-csi receiver, or with the BlueBod sketch in firmware/, then connect it over USB or Bluetooth. Three boards around the room work best.

Bluetooth notifications carry RuView CSI frames. Other byte formats are stored in exports as raw sensor data.

Link other devices over LAN ＋

A creates an offer → B pastes it and sends back its answer → A applies the answer. Linked devices share their boards' CSI, so each one adds more links across the room.
